ABOUTme!

I'm Laura — a new grad product manager who loves the messy middle between "what do people actually need?" and "it's live."

I studied at RIT, where I learned to pair systems thinking with empathy for users. I've led student product teams, run interviews and usability tests, prioritized backlogs, and partnered with engineers to ship features people use.

Outside of work: zines, record digging, film photography, and wandering new cities with a notebook.

  • FOCUS Consumer & B2B product, 0→1
  • TOOLS Figma, Jira, SQL, Amplitude, Notion
  • STATUS Open to new grad APM / PM roles
